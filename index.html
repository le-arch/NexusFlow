Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll pro-rate your billing accordingly. No penalties, no questions asked.

Is there a free trial available?

Absolutely. Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial that gives you full access to all features. No credit card is required to start your trial.

What kind of support do you offer?

Starter plans include email support with a 24-hour response time. Growth plans get priority support with a 4-hour response window. Enterprise customers receive a dedicated account manager and 1-hour response SLA.

How does the "automation runs" limit work?

An automation run is counted each time a workflow executes from start to finish. If your usage exceeds your plan limit, workflows continue to run and we'll notify you to upgrade. No hard cutoffs — your business never stops.
